Our Story

A neighbourhood pharmacy that never stopped listening

Our founder, Dr. Anjali Rao, opened the first Emeravita counter after watching families struggle to find genuine medicines and clear advice. She stocked fewer brands, checked every batch herself, and took time to explain each prescription.

That habit of slowing down for people became our culture. As we grew online, we kept it: every order is still checked by a registered pharmacist, and every question still reaches a real person.

Medicine works best when people feel calm and understood. That is the balance we are here to give.

Dr. Anjali Rao, Founder & Chief Pharmacist
